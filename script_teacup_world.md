# 🎬 PART 4 — Shot-by-Shot Direction

## 🎬 SHOT 1 — DAWN (0.0–5.0s)

**Frame:** Macro. A cream ceramic teacup on a wooden windowsill. Morning haze outside. Inside the cup: tiny mountains, a mirror-still lake, mossy shore. Everything is cool blue-grey — pre-sunrise.

**Camera:** Very slow push-in (3%), eye-level with the cup rim.

**Light:** Cold ambient, one warm glint on the rim.

**Text:** none — pure image.

**Sound:** 🎵 music box note 1 · 🐦 one distant bird · 🌬️ room tone

> 💡 **Hidden detail #1:** a tiny wooden boat on the lake (only 12 pixels wide). Nobody sees it first time.

---

## 🎬 SHOT 2 — SUNRISE (5.0–10.0s)

**Frame:** The tiny sun crests the miniature mountain ridge. **Golden light sweeps across the cup** — first the peaks, then the lake, then the moss. Long shadows stretch.

**Camera:** Almost static, 1% drift. Let the light move, not the camera.

**Light:** Transition from cool to warm — this is the color-script pivot.

**Sound:** 🎵 melody enters · ✨ soft shimmer as light hits the water

> 💡 **Hidden detail #2:** a tiny bird silhouette crosses the sun for 4 frames.

---

## 🎬 SHOT 3 — STEAM (10.0–15.0s)

**Frame:** Steam rises from the tea surface in slow curls. As it rises past the cup rim, tiny floating particles gather — the steam **thickens into a small cloud** hovering over the miniature world.

**Camera:** Slow rise, tilting up with the steam, then settling.

**Sound:** 💨 gentle steam hiss · 🎵 bar 5

> 💡 **Hidden detail #3:** the steam briefly forms a heart shape (7 frames).

---

## 🎬 SHOT 4 — FIRST DROP (15.0–20.0s)

**Frame:** The cloud turns from white to soft grey. **One raindrop falls in slow motion** — we follow it all the way down (this is the shot people replay).

**Camera:** Tilt down, tracking the drop. Slight speed ramp (0.5x → 0.35x at impact).

**Sound:** 🤫 near silence (only a low pad) — **then** 💧 a single crystalline "plink".

> 🎧 **Sound trick:** 2.5 seconds of near-silence before the drop. The brain leans in.
> This is the most replayed moment in the entire video — and it costs nothing.

---

## 🎬 SHOT 5 — RAIN (20.0–25.0s)

**Frame:** Gentle rain over the tiny world. Ripples spread on the lake. Moss darkens. The tiny boat rocks. Everything glistens.

**Camera:** Slow orbit (10°) — the only orbiting shot; it feels like relief after stillness.

**Sound:** 🌧️ soft rain (wide stereo) · 🎵 bar 9 · 🛶 faint creak of the boat

> 💡 **Hidden detail #4:** the boat's sail has a tiny stitched star.

---

## 🎬 SHOT 6 — THE RIVER (25.0–30.0s)

**Frame:** The lake overflows. A thin stream runs between the moss and spills over the cup's inner edge — **back into the tea**. The cycle completes physically.

**Camera:** Follow the water, tilt down along the cup's inner wall.

**Sound:** 💧 water trickle · 🎵 bar 11 · 🍵 ceramic resonance (low)

> 💡 **Hidden detail #5:** for 6 frames, the water forms the shape of a fish.

---

## 🎬 SHOT 7 — CLEARING (30.0–35.0s)

**Frame:** Rain stops. Mist lifts. The sun is bright again — higher, softer. Steam resumes. The cup looks warm and safe.

**Camera:** Slow pull-back, revealing the windowsill and the morning light outside.

**Sound:** 🎵 bar 13 · ✨ shimmer · 🐦 the same bird from Shot 1

> 💡 **Hidden detail #6:** on the windowsill, a second, smaller cup — empty.

---

## 🎬 SHOT 8 — THE RETURN (35.0–40.0s)

**Frame:** Camera settles back to the **exact framing of Shot 1**. The light cools. The lake stills. The steam thins to nothing. The sun sinks behind the ridge until only a glint remains on the rim — **the glint from frame 0**.

**Camera:** Static. Eases to a complete stop at 39.5s so the loop has zero movement at the seam.

**Sound:** 🎵 bar 15–16 = **exact copy of bars 1–2** · silence at 39.9s for 2 frames

> 🔁 **THE SEAM:** frame 959 and frame 0 must be *pixel-identical*.
> Verify: `python3 verify_loop.py` → score must be **≥ 0.995**.

# 🎵 PART 5 — Sound Design (this is where loops win or die)

## The Shepard illusion
Use a **Shepard tone** (an audio illusion that seems to rise forever) layered under
the last 8 bars. The music *feels* like it's climbing but ends exactly where it began.
Combined with the visual sunrise, the brain reads "forever".

## Bar map
```
Bar 1–2   : music box motif (C–E–G–E)         | dawn, still
Bar 3–4   : celesta + warm pad                 | sunrise
Bar 5–6   : breathy flute, steam texture        | steam
Bar 7–8   : low pad, drums out                  | first drop (silence at 17.5s)
Bar 9–10  : soft rain percussion, strings        | rain
Bar 11–12 : water bells, low cello              | river
Bar 13–14 : motif returns, brighter              | clearing
Bar 15–16 : EXACT COPY of bars 1–2               | return → loop
```

## Rules
1. **Bar 16 = Bar 1** (note for note). No exceptions.
2. **No fade-out.** Ever. A fade is a scar on a loop.
3. **Last 2 frames = silence** so the first note lands clean.
4. **Rain loops at 5s** (120 frames) — a hidden micro-rhythm.
5. **Master to −14 LUFS** (platform standard) so it doesn't sound quiet next to others.

# 🛠️ PART 8 — Post-Production Pipeline

```bash
# 1 — Join (frame-accurate)
printf "file 'shot1.mp4'\nfile 'shot2.mp4'\nfile 'shot3.mp4'\nfile 'shot4.mp4'\nfile 'shot5.mp4'\nfile 'shot6.mp4'\nfile 'shot7.mp4'\nfile 'shot8.mp4'\n" > clips.txt
ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i clips.txt -c copy teacup_raw.mp4

# 2 — Verify the seam (must be ≥ 0.995)
python3 verify_loop.py teacup_raw.mp4

# 3 — Cinematic grade: film grain + gentle warmth + soft bloom
ffmpeg -i teacup_raw.mp4 -vf "\
eq=contrast=1.04:saturation=1.06:brightness=0.01,\
colorbalance=rs=0.02:gs=0.005:bs=-0.02,\
noise=alls=4:allf=t,\
unsharp=5:5:0.4:5:5:0.0" \
-c:v libx264 -crf 17 -preset slow -pix_fmt yuv420p teacup_graded.mp4

# 4 — Add audio with a HARD CUT (no -shortest fades)
ffmpeg -i teacup_graded.mp4 -i score.wav -map 0:v -map 1:a \
-c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k teacup_final.mp4

# 5 — Repeat to raise watch time (4 loops = 2m40s)
python3 make_loop.py teacup_final.mp4 --repeat 4 -out teacup_2min.mp4
```
