# 🌱 "Little Seed" — A Dreamy Seamless Loop
## English · Cinematic · Beauty-First Script

**Format:** 9:16 Vertical (Shorts/Reels) — 16:9 version notes included
**Duration:** 30.0 seconds (720 frames @ 24fps)
**Tempo:** 96 BPM → 48 beats → 12 bars (perfect grid)
**Audience:** 2–6 years + parents (calming, aesthetic, "bedtime" content)
**Vibe:** dreamy, soft, golden-hour, Studio-Ghibli-meets-Pixar
**Loop type:** Circular story (seed → flower → seed)

---

# 🎨 PART 1 — The Beauty Formula (why this will look premium)

| Element | Choice | Why it looks beautiful |
|---|---|---|
| **Palette** | Dusty rose `#F2C6B4`, sage `#A8C3A0`, cream `#FFF4E6`, honey `#E8B04B` | Soft, warm, cohesive — no harsh primaries |
| **Light** | Low golden sun from back-left, **rim light** on every edge | Creates glow + depth instantly |
| **Lens** | 50mm equivalent, **f/1.8 shallow depth of field** | Creamy background bokeh |
| **Particles** | Floating dust motes + pollen sparkles | Adds magic, hides AI imperfections |
| **Motion** | Slow, easing push-ins (2–4% per shot) | Calm, hypnotic, loop-friendly |
| **Grade** | Slight lift in blacks, soft highlights, +8 warmth | Film-like, dreamy |
| **Grain** | 3% fine film grain | Kills the "plastic AI" look |
| **Sound** | Music box + celesta + soft strings, gentle rain | ASMR-soft, rewatch-friendly |

> 🎯 **Rule:** every shot must have **one** glowing element (sun, sparkle, dewdrop). The eye follows light — this is what makes it feel "expensive".

---

# 🎬 PART 2 — Story (one line)

**A tiny glowing seed sleeps in the soil → rain wakes it → it grows → blooms → releases one new seed → the new seed falls back into the soil → loop.**

Circular by nature: the ending *is* the beginning.

## 🎬 SHOT 1 — The Sleep (0.0 – 5.0s)

**Visual:** Extreme macro. Dark, rich soil. In the center, one **tiny seed glowing warm gold**. A single dewdrop falls from above in slow motion, lands beside it, ripples.

**Camera:** Very slow push-in (3%). Static otherwise.

**Text:** `one little seed` — lowercase, thin serif, cream color, fades in at 1.0s, out at 4.0s.

**Sound:** 🎵 music box first note · 💧 single water drop · 🌬️ soft breeze

---

## 🎬 SHOT 2 — Wake Up (5.0 – 10.0s)

**Visual:** The seed cracks. A **pale green sprout** pushes up through the soil. Tiny sparkles rise with it. Sun rays break through from the top-left, backlighting the sprout.

**Camera:** Macro tilt-up following the sprout. Constant speed.

**Text:** `wake up` (fades in 6.0s, out 8.5s)

**Sound:** 🌱 soft crackle · ✨ rising chime (glissando) · 🎵 music box melody continues

---

## 🎬 SHOT 3 — Grow (10.0 – 15.0s)

**Visual:** Time-lapse feel (but smooth, not jerky). Two round leaves **unfurl**, a small bud forms at the top. Dust motes float across the frame, catching the light.

**Camera:** Gentle 15° orbit around the plant, slow.

**Text:** `grow` (10.5s – 13.0s)

**Sound:** 🍃 leaf unfurl · 🎻 soft strings enter · 🎵 melody rises

---

## 🎬 SHOT 4 — Bloom (15.0 – 20.0s)

**Visual:** ⭐ **The beauty shot.** The bud opens into a **soft peach-pink flower**. Petals unfold one by one in slow motion. A gentle glow pulse travels outward. Background bokeh blooms with golden light.

**Camera:** Slow push-in + slight rise (5%). This is the emotional peak.

**Text:** `bloom` (15.5s – 18.5s)

**Sound:** 🎵 full melody · ✨ shimmer swell · 🌸 soft "opening" whoosh

---

## 🎬 SHOT 5 — Release (20.0 – 25.0s)

**Visual:** The flower tilts. A **single glowing seed** drifts out and falls slowly (slow motion, 0.3x speed). The camera tracks the seed as it spirals down toward the soil. Flower softens into bokeh behind.

**Camera:** Tilt-down tracking the seed, smooth.

**Text:** `again` (21.0s – 24.0s)

**Sound:** 🎵 melody softens · ✨ single sustained chime following the fall · 💧 tiny drop at landing

---

## 🎬 SHOT 6 — The Return (25.0 – 30.0s)

**Visual:** The seed settles into the soil — **exactly where Shot 1's seed was**. The soil closes around it. The glow returns to the same gold. Dust motes settle.

**Camera:** Static — settles on the **exact same frame as 0.000s**.

**Text:** none (keep the loop clean)

**Sound:** 🎵 final bar = **exact copy of bar 1** · 🌬️ breeze fades into the same breeze that opens the video

> 🔁 **LOOP POINT:** last frame = first frame, last sound = first sound. Viewer never notices.

# 🎵 PART 4 — Original English Lyrics

**Style:** gentle sing-song, whispered, ASMR-like — **not** shouty kids' music.
**Instrumentation:** music box, celesta, soft strings, rain, birds far away.

```
[Verse 1 — Shots 1–2]
One little seed, asleep in the ground,
Wake up, wake up, without a sound.
Rain falls down, the sun says hello,
Grow, little seed, grow, grow, grow.

[Verse 2 — Shots 3–4]
Leaves unfold, a flower appears,
Soft and bright, it smiles through the years.
Petals open, catching the light,
Bloom, little flower, bloom in the light.
```

> 💡 The last word **"more"** lands on the beat where the music box plays its first note again —
> so the loop feels like the song never ended.

# 🛠️ PART 7 — Post-Production

## Assemble + verify loop
```bash
# 1. join shots
ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i clips.txt -c copy little_seed.mp4

# 2. verify seamlessness
python3 verify_loop.py little_seed.mp4     # target: 0.99+

# 3. gentle color grade (dreamy warm)
ffmpeg -i little_seed.mp4 -vf "eq=contrast=1.03:saturation=1.05:brightness=0.02,colorbalance=rs=0.03:gs=0.01:bs=-0.03,noise=alls=3:allf=t" -c:v libx264 -crf 18 graded.mp4

# 4. add music with a hard cut (no fade out!)
ffmpeg -i graded.mp4 -i music.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest final.mp4

# 5. repeat for watch-time
python3 make_loop.py final.mp4 --repeat 4
```

## ⚠️ Grade rules
- **Do NOT fade out audio.** Fade = broken loop.
- Keep the **first and last 0.5s un-graded differently** — same filter on the whole clip.
- Export at **-crf 18** or lower (quality matters for this style).
